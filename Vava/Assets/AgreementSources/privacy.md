# Vava Privacy Policy

November 13, 2026 | Draft for review

## Local profile and activity

Vava stores a device-local profile identifier, your profile details, consent, notes, preferences and practice progress in its app container. The app uses these records to restore your activity on this device.

## Video access

When you select a video through the iOS photo picker, Vava can read the selected file and make a copy for local playback. Vava does not upload your selected video. The original remains in Photos when you remove Vava's copy.

## External services

Replay and coaching links open publisher sites. Those sites process requests under their own privacy policies. Apple processes in-app purchases when available. Vava stores verified credits, spending and course access in the local wallet.

## Your choices

Use Settings to edit your profile or delete your local profile and activity. Use Me to remove imported copies. If you use the iOS share sheet, the selected destination receives the items you choose to share.
