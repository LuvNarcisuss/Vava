# Vava Terms of Service

November 13, 2026 | Draft for review

## The app

Vava provides table tennis clips, practice plans and links to publisher replays. Publisher sites apply their own terms and availability.

## Local use

Your profile, notes, saved items, training progress and imported videos stay in Vava's local library. You can remove local videos or delete the local profile in Settings. Deleting a copy does not change the original in Photos.

## Your content

Use videos and other material you have permission to use. Vava creates a local copy when you import a video. You can choose to share items through iOS.

## Coins and lessons

Apple processes coin purchases when available. Coins unlock eligible practice plans, have no cash value and do not expire. Vava stores the wallet and course access on this device. Deleting the profile removes access to its local wallet and lessons. Apple handles purchases and refunds through its own channels.
