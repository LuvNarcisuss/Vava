# Product scope

Vava follows Toli's five independent navigation stacks and its compact editorial layout. The main navigation opens on its first tab, Academy. The Release Bundle ID is `com.vava.pingpong`. Debug testing uses `im.seex.sao2` with the Yu Sun team.

| Screen | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Academy | Search and filter training courses. Tap the lesson area to open its details or the avatar and name to open its credited coaching source. Report lessons or block a coaching source from the lesson menu. Read public coaching links. Unlock original exercises once with coins, save courses, and complete practice steps. |
| Watch | Tap anywhere on a match card to open the replay details. Filter, save, read credits, and open the publisher's original replay with internet access. |
| Shorts | One real match recording at a time. Drag vertically to page through videos, expand search above the dimmed feed, tap the video to play or pause, double-tap to like, save, share, read Vava Editorial notes and add dated private comments or replies in half-height comments with playback underneath, visit publisher profiles, read credits, report clips, block publishers, and hide clips locally. Import videos through Photos and keep the copies on this device. |
| Chats | Show a welcome card for an empty friend list. Save friends by name and email, open their conversations, keep drafts, and continue writing in Mail. |
| Me | View identity, engagement totals for local works, the following count, and Shorts, Tutorials, Liked, and Saved collections. Practice progress appears under Tutorials. Edit identity, log out, or delete the account in Settings. Open Wallet to buy coin packs and review purchases and spending. |

Pull down at the top of a Vava screen to refresh. Shorts advances to a different available video, and content lists rotate eligible results within the current filter. Comments refresh private replies and rotate editorial notes. Details, forms, and empty states refresh their current data and acknowledge completion. Wallet refreshes the current storefront prices.

Use plain English for product actions, availability, and errors. Keep storage and development environment labels in engineering documentation. Describe private comments as private and keep editorial attribution visible.

Evergreen table textures, vermilion accents and ivory lettering support the table-tennis editorial design. Kalam Bold is the display typeface. Comic Neue is the reading typeface. Third-party and operating-system surfaces control their own rendering.

The app opens after accepting the agreements and uses the on-device library. Agreement links open the owner-provided Google Docs. StoreKit and publisher links use their respective online services.

Wallet shows nine consumable coin packs in a three-column grid ordered by reference USD price. Accessibility text sizes use a single column. Choose an available pack, then tap Buy to open Apple's purchase sheet. Prices come from the current App Store storefront. Pull down to reload prices when an offer is unavailable.

Publisher profiles show the credited source and use the same coaching photo as the lesson cards. Recording sources use a still from a credited work. Their engagement totals sum every clip credited to the same publisher. Following, blocking and private comments stay on this device. Publisher profiles have separate Shorts and Tutorials collections. Message opens a private draft. Manage blocked publishers in Settings.
