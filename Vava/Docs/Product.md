# Product scope

Vava follows Toli's five independent navigation stacks and its compact editorial layout. The Bundle ID is `com.vava.pingpong`. The signing team and StoreKit product identifiers await the owner.

| Screen | Behavior |
| --- | --- |
| Shorts | One real match recording at a time. Swipe, search, play and pause, mute, like, save, share, discuss with replies, read credits, and hide clips locally. Import videos through Photos and keep the copies on this device. |
| Academy | Search and filter training courses. Read public coaching links. Unlock original exercises once with coins, save courses, and complete practice steps. |
| Watch | Browse local replay cards, filter, save, read credits, and open the publisher's original replay with internet access. |
| Chats | Show that connected messaging is unavailable. |
| Me | View the local profile, imported videos, likes, saves and practice progress. Edit identity in Settings. Open Wallet to review purchases and spending. |

Evergreen table textures, vermilion accents and ivory lettering support the table-tennis editorial design. Kalam Bold is the display typeface. Comic Neue is the reading typeface. Third-party and operating-system surfaces control their own rendering.

The app opens after accepting the bundled agreements and uses the on-device library. StoreKit and publisher links use their respective online services.
